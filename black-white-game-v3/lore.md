# Bringer of Light

## The Dimming

Long ago, the world was lit by the **Eternal Flame** — a living light kept in the heart of a vast underground city. Civilizations thrived in its glow. Markets bustled in golden warmth, children played in corridors that never knew night, and the people believed the light would last forever.

But something consumed it. The people called it *The Dimming* — not a creature, not a storm, but an **absence**. Light simply… stopped. Torches refused to catch. Lanterns flickered and died. The Eternal Flame shrank to an ember, then to nothing.

The city fell silent. Its people vanished — not dead, just *gone*, as if light was what held them together. What remained were walls, relics, and darkness so deep it felt alive.

---

## The Spark

You are a **spark** — the last ember that escaped the Eternal Flame the moment before it was swallowed. You don't remember what you are. You don't know why you glow. You only know one thing: **move forward**.

When you touch the old torches, they remember fire. When you find the relics, the world remembers itself — fragments of frozen memory play out before you: a merchant mid-sentence, a child's drawing on a wall, a warning carved too late.

---

## The Relics

Each relic is a **fragment of memory** — a moment captured in light at the instant of The Dimming. They hold the truth of what happened:

- **Merchant's Ledger** — *"The shadow merchants offered a trade. Endless night for endless wealth. We should have known..."*
- **Child's Drawing** — A crude sketch of a sun with a face. Below it: *"When does it come back?"*
- **Captain's Warning** — *"Seal the lower vaults. Whatever is down there, it isn't darkness. Darkness doesn't breathe."*
- **Scholar's Notes** — *"The Flame isn't dying. It's being eaten. Something learned to feed on light itself."*
- **Last Record** — *"If you're reading this, you are the light now. Don't go deeper. Don't—"*

---

## The Truth

The deeper you go, the more you learn: **The Dimming wasn't an accident.** Someone chose darkness. A pact was made — light for power — and the cost was everything.

And they're still down here. Waiting. Not in the shadows.

*They are the shadows.*
